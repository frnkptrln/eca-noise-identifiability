# Lead's additional search for item A21 (2026-11-14)

Purpose: the literature check (`../literature/literature_check.md`, finding L2) attributes the statement
"only states in a locally stable component can carry weight in the limit distribution" to Noeldeke and
Samuelson (1993, Proposition 1) on the basis of secondary descriptions. One web search was run by the lead
to see whether that secondary description can be found independently.

Query: Noeldeke Samuelson 1993 "locally stable component" stochastically stable single mutation absorbing
sets Proposition 1

Secondary sources returned that describe the proposition (none is the primary text; none was read beyond
the search extract):

1. J. Oechssler, lecture slides "Stochastic Stability", University of Heidelberg, 13 November 2013
   (uni-heidelberg.de/md/awi/forschung/stochastic_stability_slides.pdf): states a lemma on
   single-mutation neighbourhoods, defines mutation-connected components, and states the proposition with
   the attribution Noeldeke/Samuelson (1993).
2. S. Huck and J. Oechssler, "The Indirect Evolutionary Approach to Explaining Fair Allocations"
   (working paper, econwpa 9507001): cites Proposition 1 of Noeldeke and Samuelson (1993) in a proof and
   defines "component" and "locally stable" (at least two mutations to reach any other component).
3. A. B. Ania, T. Troeger, A. Wambach, "An Evolutionary Analysis of Insurance Markets with Adverse
   Selection": cites Noeldeke and Samuelson (1993, Proposition 1) for the long-run set being a union of
   locally stable components.

Result: three secondary sources agree with the description used in L2. The primary text was not obtained,
so item A21 stays provisional.
