## (A) Errors found

A1. FALSE CLAIM (Section 3.2, line 149; Figure 2 caption): "8 of the rising classes (rules 15, 51, 60, 90, 105, 150, 170, 204) are the rules whose global map is a bijection of S: for them the uniform law is stationary at every eps>0". On 12 cells only rules 15, 51, 85, 170, 204, 240 (classes 15, 51, 170, 204) are bijective. Own scan of all 256 rules: |F(S)| = 4096 for 15, 51, 170, 204; 2048 for rule 60 (F(2047)=F(2048)=2049); 1024 for 90, 105, 150 (rule 90: F(341)=F(1024)=2560). Uniform is not stationary: ||u - uP||_1 = 0.282 (rule 60, eps 0.05), 0.673 (rule 90), 0.646 (105, 150); stationary law has max|pi-1/4096| = 1.05e-3 for rule 90 at eps 0.05. The repository's own file shows it: stationary_rates.csv min_pi at eps=0.001 is 3.48e-8 (rule 90), 2.54e-9 (105, 150), 2.72e-7 (60) versus 2.441e-4 for the four real bijections. What IS true: nu_c = 1.5 for every code for all eight rules at every grid point (max|nu-1.5| <= 4.2e-12 in the file, confirmed by my solves), so rho = 1.5 lambda(eps) and the curves coincide; for the additive rules 60/90/105/150 the reason is uniform 3-cell marginals (Fourier coefficients of pi vanish off the generalised kernel of L^T, which contains no vector supported on 3 adjacent cells), not a uniform law. Pipeline cause: make_numbers.py lines 136-138 define BijectiveRules as "nu_c = 1.5 at eps 0.1", which tests marginals, not bijectivity.

A2. Lemma 3 statement (line 90), last clause "matching KL = infinity at eps = 0 for every g != f": false as stated. At eps = 0 and fixed x_0, E_T(f||g; x_0, 0) = 0 whenever g agrees with f on the codes visited before T; the note's own Section 1.4 counts 33.1% of (f, x_0) pairs for which this holds for all T. For rule 204 it holds for 3316 of 4096 initial states. The lemma is listed in Section 5 as "proved for all n".

A3. Lemma 3 (line 90) says the curve "has an interior maximum eps*(f)"; only existence of an interior maximiser is proved, uniqueness is not (the note itself says unimodality is observed, not proved), so eps*(f) is not well defined by the lemma. The proof's first sentence ("continuity of pi -> pi P_{f,eps} in eps") is garbled; what is used is continuity of eps -> P_{f,eps} plus compactness of the simplex.

A4. Line 137 "The best class identifies the rule from 99.8% of initial states (rule 25)": classes 25 and 60 tie exactly (4086/4096 = 99.76% each, zero_noise_per_rule.csv and own recomputation); the macro ZeroBestClassRule uses idxmax and drops rule 60.

A5. Line 161 finite-horizon percentages for "the initial states that are permanently non-identifiable at eps=0" are means over the 88 classes of per-class fractions (only the figure caption says so). The pooled fraction of such states differs: at T=32, pooled vs class-averaged = 51.8 vs 63.1% (eps 0.005), 61.4 vs 71.9 (0.01), 77.3 vs 84.2 (0.05), 85.6 vs 90.3 (0.1), 93.2 vs 95.7 (0.2); classes have between 10 and 3316 such states. For "all initial states" the two coincide. The body text should say which it reports.

A6. Section 5: "the classification of the 46 classes ... rests on the trend at eps in [0.001, 0.002]": not for rules 7, 23, 232, whose smallest grid entries are unresolved; their trend ratios come from eps 0.02/0.03 (rule 7) and 0.01/0.02 (23, 232). Same for FallingBTrendMin = 1.662 (rule 19, eps 0.01/0.02). Conclusions unaffected (all are falling classes with interior maxima).

A7. Abstract (line 31): the range "eps* in [0.010, 0.293]" is attached to the sentence about the 42 B classes, but is the range over the 61 falling classes (42 B + 19 U); over the 42 B classes alone it is [0.088, 0.293] (rules 26, 128); the 0.010 is rule 106 (U; raw 0.0095). "we locate eps* for every class" is not what was done: eps* was located for the 62 classes with an interior grid maximum; 26 are reported as "-> 0".

A8. Line 151 says Figure 1(b) covers "these 61 classes"; make_figures.py plots all 62 classes with interior_max == 1, including rule 41 (dagger), visible at (0.99, 0.035). The Spearman values are over 61.

A9. Line 125 "about an hour on two CPU cores": the two stationary logs alone end at 2039 s and 1779 s (64 min); refine, finite-horizon, zero-noise and radius-2 stages come on top (their logs carry no timings).

A10. Minor: Remark 1 "the classes of 1.4 are unchanged" under a readout channel holds for a channel injective on laws (BSC with eps<1/2), not for every K. Symmetry check: 5.9e-11 is the comparison non-representative vs representative (reproduced: rules 179 vs 50, comp, eps 0.001); including self-symmetries of self-symmetric rules the worst deviation is 2.5e-10 (rule 232, self-complementary, eps 0.001, an unresolved entry); over resolved entries 5.9e-11 again.
