## Notes

* The task text describes Theorem A as a restatement of the single-mutation-chain lemma. The repository documents
  are more specific: the first sentence of Theorem A, with Lemmas 1 and 2, corresponds to the results on
  mutation-connected and locally stable components (finding A21, `VERIFICATION_ring_size.md`:274); the chain lemma corresponds to
  Lemma B (finding A20, `VERIFICATION_ring_size.md`:267) and to its weaker form (`report_ring_size.md`:217). `related_work.tex` follows the
  documents.
* Both attributions are provisional in the repository, because the primary texts of Samuelson and of Noeldeke and
  Samuelson were not read (`VERIFICATION_ring_size.md`:382; `verify_ring_size/literature/literature_check.md`:76; `literature_ring_size.md`:26).
* The lemma quoted by Ellison has a radius condition, printed "R(Omega) > 2" in the scan; the reading notes say that
  ">=" cannot be excluded at the resolution of the scan (`verify_ring_size/literature/ellison2000_published_reading_notes.md`:58), and the literature check adds that with
  CR* = 1 Theorem 2 itself needs only R(Omega) > 1 (`verify_ring_size/literature/literature_check.md`:69). The report's short form (`report_ring_size.md`:91, next
  lines) omits the radius condition; `related_work.tex` keeps it ("satisfies a radius condition").
* Ellison attributes the lemma to Noeldeke and Samuelson "(1993, 1997)" (`verify_ring_size/literature/ellison2000_published_reading_notes.md`:56). The repository has no
  bibliographic data for the paper of 1997, so `refs.bib` has no entry for it.
